POS Product Card Info

Cost and on-hand quantity on every POS product card

Adds a small information icon to the top-right corner of every POS product card. Tapping it reveals the product's cost and on-hand quantity in a compact popover without leaving the product screen.

Independently Toggleable

Enable Show Cost and Show On-hand Qty separately from the Point of Sale settings, per point of sale.

Variant Aware

For multi-variant products, the popover lists cost and on-hand quantity per variant so cashiers see the correct stock and cost.

Lightweight Load

On-hand quantity is only loaded into the POS session when the option is enabled, keeping session start fast when the feature is off.

Cashier Friendly

Cost and stock stay one tap away on the product grid — no need to open backend product forms during a sale.

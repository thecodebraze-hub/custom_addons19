Bar Liquor Inventory

Track opened bottles and shot consumption in your bar POS

Built for bars and restaurants that sell liquor by the bottle and by the shot. Mark liquor products once, track sealed stock plus the active opened bottle balance in millilitres (ml), and let the POS automatically open bottles and deduct consumption when shot products are sold.

Key Features

Is Liquor flag on products
Tick Is Liquor on the product form (next to Sales / Point of Sale / Purchase). Liquor settings appear only for liquor products — other items stay clean.

Full bottle tracking
Set bottle size (e.g. 750 ml), view sealed stock quantity, remaining ml on the active opened bottle, and a combined display such as 2 Bottles + 350ml.

Opened bottle register
Every opened bottle is recorded under Inventory → Opened Liquor Bottles with size, remaining ml, state, and linked POS order.

Shot products
Create shot/service products (e.g. 100 ml, 200 ml) linked to a parent full bottle. Define consumption ml per unit sold — ideal for arrack, whisky, vodka, and spirits.

Automatic POS consumption
When a shot line is paid in POS, the module opens a sealed bottle if needed and deducts the correct ml from the active opened bottle. Stock moves are created automatically.

Variant support
Liquor settings on the product template apply to all variants, so every size or attribute variant is handled consistently in POS.

How It Works

  1. Install the module and open a full bottle product (e.g. Gal Arrack 750 ml).
  2. Tick Is Liquor and set Bottle Size (ml).
  3. Create shot products — tick Is Shot Product, select the parent bottle, and set Consumption ML per sale.
  4. Receive sealed bottles into stock as usual via purchase or inventory adjustments.
  5. In POS, sell shot products — on payment the system opens a bottle when needed and reduces remaining ml.
  6. Monitor open bottles and balances from the product form or Opened Liquor Bottles menu.

Product Setup

Field Where Purpose
Is Liquor Product form Enables liquor tracking fields
Bottle Size (ml) Product form Full bottle capacity in millilitres
Is Shot Product Product form Marks a saleable shot / pour product
Parent Bottle / Consumption ML Shot product Links shot to bottle and sets ml deducted per unit
